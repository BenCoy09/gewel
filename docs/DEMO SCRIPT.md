# Demo video: 3 minutes

**Rule:** real screen captures of the real app, recorded on a phone where possible. Motion goes into titles and transitions only. Nothing on screen that you have not run for real.

| Time | On screen | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Black. One line fades in: "January 1994. The CFA franc loses half its value overnight." Cut to your real storyteller's face or hands (with consent). | "The people who lived it rarely get to tell it. And when they do, nothing proves it's theirs." |
| 0:20–0:35 | Gewel logo, tagline. | "Gewel. A modern griot. Stories of money, signed by the people who lived them." |
| 0:35–1:05 | Phone: join (two fields), recovery key screen, record a short story in Wolof or French, tap Sign. | "No email, no password. The key is born in the browser. The recording is fingerprinted and signed before it leaves the phone." |

| 1:50–2:15 | Clear browser data, paste recovery key, stories reappear. | "Lose your phone, keep your history." |
| 2:15–2:50 | Add Lightning address, open the story link, pay a real invoice from a real wallet, show the sats arrive. | "Listeners pay from their own wallet. The sats go straight to the storyteller. We never hold money." |
| 2:50–3:00 | Real storyteller line (their words), then the URL and repo. | One sentence from them on why this matters. End on the link. |

## Before you record
- Warm up the Render server (open the site 2 minutes earlier).
- Use a clean browser profile, light theme, large text, notifications off.
- Record voiceover separately, in a quiet room, then edit to it.
- Subtitles in English and French.
- Export 1080p, under 3:00, and watch it once on a phone.

## Cut if unverified
OpenTimestamps, AI transcripts, the wallet panel. Show them only if you have run them for real. A short demo that works beats a long one that might not.
